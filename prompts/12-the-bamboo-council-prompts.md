- **Primary Style:** Anime/manga inspired with cyberpunk elements

- **Format:** High-resolution digital illustrations

- **Resolution:** Minimum 1920x1080 for scenes, 1080x1350 for portraits
- **Style Consistency:** Maintain uniform art style across all images
